- redistribute an unmodified official release while preserving its notices.

- present a modified fork as the official Kavox project;
- use Kavox branding in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation;

from Kavox. This policy should be reviewed by qualified trademark counsel before
formal enforcement or trademark registration.
