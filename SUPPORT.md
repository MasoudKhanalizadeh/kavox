# Support and contact

Use the channel that matches the request:

- **Bug report:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/MasoudKhanalizadeh/kavox/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Usage question or feature idea:** [open a question](https://github.com/MasoudKhanalizadeh/kavox/issues/new?template=question.yml).
- **Security or data-safety concern:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid public disclosure of sensitive details.
- **Private or commercial inquiry:** use the contact information published on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MasoudKhanalizadeh).

Before submitting an issue, remove hostnames, customer data, credentials, serial
numbers, WWNs, filesystem UUIDs, and other infrastructure identifiers from logs
and result files.

Kavox is maintained on a best-effort basis. Opening an issue does not create a
service-level or response-time commitment.
